## Effective Service Provision

### Accuracy of Advertising and Notice of Insured Status
- **Jurisdiction:** US Federal — National Credit Union Administration
- **Source:** [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/16/2026-23288/accuracy-of-advertising-and-notice-of-insured-status)
- **Status:** Rule (as of 2026-11-16)
- **Summary:** The NCUA Board finalized a rule eliminating provisions related to the official advertising statement requirements for federally insured credit unions, while leaving official sign display requirements untouched. The change is meant to cut regulatory complexity and reduce compliance burden and costs for credit unions, giving them more flexibility in advertising.
- **Why it matters:** A modest deregulatory streamlining that reduces compliance overhead for thousands of credit unions, though it has limited direct public impact beyond cost savings for regulated entities.

### C14-2026-0046 - Howard Plaza - Conduct a public hearing and approve an ordinance amending City Code Title 25 by rezoning property locally known as 3127 East Howard Lane (Harris Branch Watershed).…
- **Jurisdiction:** Austin, TX
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://austintexas.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=29614&GUID=5A2FFA79-2D75-421D-80B6-6FC0427638FE)
- **Status:** Agenda Ready (as of 2026-11-20)
- **Summary:** The Austin City Council is set to hold a public hearing and vote on rezoning 3127 East Howard Lane from warehouse/limited office to community commercial (GR-CO) zoning, following a recommendation from the Zoning and Platting Commission. The change would allow more intensive commercial development on the site, with a conditional overlay to address site-specific concerns. This is a routine but concrete case of the city loosening land-use restrictions to enable more development on a specific parcel.
- **Why it matters:** A modest, parcel-level upzoning that modestly expands commercial development capacity in Austin, illustrative of the incremental zoning changes that cumulatively ease land-use constraints.

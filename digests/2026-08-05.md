## Energy Supply

### Generator Interconnection Programmatic Environmental Impact Statement
- **Jurisdiction:** US Federal — Tennessee Valley Authority
- **Source:** [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/20/2026-14605/generator-interconnection-programmatic-environmental-impact-statement)
- **Status:** Notice (as of 2026-07-20)
- **Summary:** The Tennessee Valley Authority announced it will prepare a Programmatic Environmental Impact Statement covering transmission activities tied to generator interconnection requests across its service area. This creates a bounded, reusable NEPA framework so individual interconnection projects can rely on tiered, site-specific reviews instead of starting environmental analysis from scratch each time. The goal is faster, more consistent, and more predictable federal environmental review for new generation connecting to TVA's grid.
- **Why it matters:** This is a meaningful process reform for one of the nation's largest public power utilities, potentially speeding up interconnection timelines for a large volume of new generation projects, especially renewables and storage, seeking to connect across the Tennessee Valley region.

## Housing Supply

### LDR-194-2026
- **Jurisdiction:** Prince George's County, MD
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://princegeorgescountymd.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=19459&GUID=823FF65A-39C1-4AF8-9BEC-D104CE92BB55)
- **Status:** introduction and referral (as of 2026-09-11)
- **Summary:** The Prince George's County Council introduced a resolution to legally bridge the gap between Maryland's state-mandated deadline for local accessory dwelling unit (ADU) laws and the actual enactment date of the County's own ADU legislation. This ensures the county remains compliant with state law while it finalizes local rules permitting ADUs like backyard cottages and basement apartments. It's a procedural but necessary step toward enabling more ADU construction countywide.
- **Why it matters:** A modest but concrete step that clears the legal path for Prince George's County homeowners to add ADUs, incrementally expanding the region's housing supply.

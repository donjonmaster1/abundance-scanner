## Housing Supply

### Single Family Housing Guaranteed Loan Program-Income Producing Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) Provisions
- **Jurisdiction:** US Federal — Agriculture Department, Rural Housing Service
- **Source:** [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/29/2026-22038/single-family-housing-guaranteed-loan-program-income-producing-accessory-dwelling-unit-adu)
- **Status:** Rule (as of 2026-10-29)
- **Summary:** USDA's Rural Housing Service finalized a rule amending its Single Family Housing Guaranteed Loan Program to permit financing of single-family homes that include income-producing accessory dwelling units, and clarified eligibility for homes with features supporting small home-based businesses. This allows rural borrowers using the guaranteed loan program to build or buy homes with rentable ADUs, adding housing units and income potential without new federal spending programs. It modernizes program rules to match how rural homeowners actually use their properties.
- **Why it matters:** A modest but meaningful regulatory fix that unlocks ADU construction financing for rural homeowners, incrementally expanding housing supply and household income options in underserved rural markets.

### ORD-MC Amend Sec 656.306 (Medium Density Residential Category), Subpt B (Residential Use Categories & Zoning Districts), Pt 3 (Schedule of District Regulations), Sec 656.432 (Applicability), Subpt C…
- **Jurisdiction:** Jacksonville, FL
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://jaxcityc.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=6895&GUID=373E7268-5F89-42C8-8CA4-02A9478BAD3B)
- **Status:** Enacted (as of 2026-10-29)
- **Summary:** The Jacksonville City Council enacted an ordinance amending its zoning code to revise regulations governing cottages and accessory dwelling units (ADUs), including updates to density categories, architectural standards, and definitions. The change streamlines rules that previously constrained these smaller housing types, making it easier to build them in medium-density residential zones. It passed after amendment through council committees despite a planning commission denial recommendation.
- **Why it matters:** A moderate but meaningful win for housing supply advocates, as easing ADU and cottage regulations in a major Florida city can unlock incremental infill housing capacity for homeowners and small developers.

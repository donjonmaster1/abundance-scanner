### LDR-186-2026
- **Jurisdiction:** Prince George's County, MD
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://princegeorgescountymd.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=19429&GUID=B6186F50-A8FA-4765-8F92-1ABCB8B82DA9)
- **Status:** Presentation and referral (as of 2026-08-31)
- **Summary:** Prince George's County, MD introduced LDR-186-2026, a comprehensive bill establishing rules for accessory dwelling units (ADUs), covering rental requirements, a ban on short-term rentals of ADUs, and standards addressing tree canopy, impervious surface, and septic system rules. The bill was presented and referred for further council review rather than adopted. It represents a structured framework intended to legalize and regulate ADUs as a form of added housing supply.
- **Why it matters:** If enacted, this would give homeowners a clear, legal path to build ADUs, modestly expanding housing supply and affordable rental options in the county.

### LDR-13-2026
- **Jurisdiction:** Prince George's County, MD
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://princegeorgescountymd.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=18675&GUID=94588756-F3C3-437A-9FC1-D82AD6C56DD9)
- **Status:** Presentation and referral (as of 2026-08-31)
- **Summary:** Prince George's County, MD introduced the Commercial Reinvestment and Innovation Projects Act of 2026, which would create a streamlined, alternative development review and appeal process for projects in designated Regional Transit Districts and Local Transit Centers under the county's Plan 2035 General Plan. The goal is to cut permitting friction and spur economic growth and development near transit hubs. This item was just presented and referred, so it is an early-stage proposal, not yet enacted.
- **Why it matters:** If passed, this could meaningfully speed up approvals for transit-oriented commercial and housing projects in a major DC-area county, benefiting developers and transit-adjacent residents alike.

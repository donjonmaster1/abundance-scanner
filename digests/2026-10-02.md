- **Jurisdiction:** Fresno, CA
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://fresno.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=21969&GUID=C3147FBA-88C1-45F6-9B89-F4A912124811)
- **Status:** Agenda Ready (as of 2026-10-01)

### AEC Rezoning
- **Jurisdiction:** Madison, WI
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://madison.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=102891&GUID=3642E96F-44EE-4532-8E9C-6882DC71B23D)
- **Status:** Council New Business (as of 2026-10-01)
- **Summary:** The Madison Common Council is considering a rezoning of a city-owned parcel at 34 Fairgrounds Drive from Parks & Recreation (PR) zoning to Regional Mixed-Use (RMX) zoning. This change would allow the site to be developed with a mix of housing, retail, and commercial uses instead of being restricted to park-related purposes. The item is before the Council as new business, with the ordinance creating two new zoning sections to implement the change.
- **Why it matters:** If approved, this rezoning removes a regulatory barrier to development on a city parcel, a modest but tangible step toward enabling new housing and mixed-use construction in Madison's District 14.

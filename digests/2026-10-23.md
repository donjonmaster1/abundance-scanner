### An ordinance amending the Somerville Zoning Ordinance to establish the Upzoning Overlay (UO) District at Davis Square, Porter Square, and Ball Square.
- **Jurisdiction:** Somerville, MA
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://somervillema.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=35231&GUID=F100F564-8842-4F89-9920-A7052BC84D68)
- **Status:** Referred for Recommendation (as of 2026-10-23)
- **Summary:** Somerville is considering an ordinance to create an Upzoning Overlay District covering the Davis Square, Porter Square, and Ball Square neighborhoods, which would allow denser residential and mixed-use development near these transit hubs. The measure has been referred for recommendation, marking a procedural step toward increasing the city's housing capacity in transit-accessible areas. If adopted, it would relax existing zoning restrictions to permit more housing units in some of the city's most walkable, transit-connected commercial districts.
- **Why it matters:** A meaningful step for a dense Boston-area suburb to legalize more housing near transit, potentially easing regional supply constraints if it clears the remaining approval process.

### Public Hearing and Ordinance Adoption - Rezoning Application Z-SP-5-23-7 - Southwest Corner of 15th Avenue and Jackson Street (Ordinance G-7167)
- **Jurisdiction:** Phoenix, AZ
- **Source:** [Legistar (municipal)](https://phoenix.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=27023&GUID=424DB530-4C00-4CAF-A84A-4A6707005878)
- **Status:** Passed (as of 2026-08-11)
- **Summary:** The Phoenix City Council adopted Ordinance G-7167, rezoning a parcel at the southwest corner of 15th Avenue and Jackson Street following a public hearing on rezoning application Z-SP-5-23-7. The change allows for higher-density development on the site than previously permitted. This is a routine but meaningful step in enabling more housing units to be built in the area.
- **Why it matters:** A modest but concrete local zoning change that removes a legal barrier to denser housing construction near downtown Phoenix, benefiting future residents and developers seeking infill projects.
